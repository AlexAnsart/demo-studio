# hello-demo

Silent preview clip for the root `README.md` — composed with `macos-dark` +
`ribbon-indigo` wallpaper.

## Show

1. Open `app/index.html`, wait for it to settle.
2. Focus the task input (composer).
3. Type "Ship the v1 release".
4. Click the priority chip — cycles to "High".
5. Click Add — expect the new item in the list.
6. Wide — let the viewer see the list update (~0.9s).
7. Focus the new item, click its checkbox to complete it, hold ~1.2s.
8. Wide (~0.7s).
9. Click the "Active" filter tab (wide).
10. Focus the filtered list — hold ~1.1s.
11. Wide (~0.6s).
12. Click delete on the remaining active item (wide).
13. Click the "All" filter tab (wide).
14. Focus the list — payoff, hold ~2.2s (both completed tasks visible).
15. Wide, hold ~1s, stop.

Four focus shots total: composer, new-item, filtered-list, payoff.
